# Label definitions for TakeMeter on r/csmajors comments, one per line, as
# `label: definition`. Same wording as the README's Label Taxonomy, so the
# baseline is judged on the definitions the labels were made with.

experience: reports something that happened to the writer, with an outcome or a measured detail such as an offer, a rejection, an invite, pay, a timeline or a count
argued: makes a claim about the field or gives advice and backs it with a reason the reader could check, such as a company practice, a recruiting step, a number, or something the writer or others saw happen
hot_take: makes a confident claim or gives advice with no checkable reason, or with a reason that is only another claim
chatter: adds no claim and no outcome, such as questions, status check-ins, support, venting and jokes
