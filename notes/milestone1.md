# Milestone 1 — community and candidate distinctions

**Community:** r/csmajors (public subreddit, no login needed to read).

**Unit: comments, not posts.** Most r/csmajors top-level posts are questions
("Capital One TDP powerday?", "What to prepare for IBM?"), so posts alone don't
vary much. The comments under them do: first-hand reports, advice, bare
opinions, status checks and venting all show up in the same thread.

**Read:** the 69 comments in `reading_sample.md` (public comments of 40+ chars,
pulled 2026-10-14).

**Left out while reading:** AutoModerator removal notices ("Your post/comment
was identified as something likely to violate rule 5...") and one comment not
in English. Neither is a take anyone wrote.

## Candidate distinctions I saw

1. **Some comments report what happened to the writer, with specifics you
   could check: dates, rounds, timelines. Most don't.**
   - "i finished the OA at 10:30 PM on the 23rd of September, recruiter emailed
     me asking for some info and scheduling on the 25th of September."
   - "it took around a month to hear back after the oa... final round and offer
     the next day"

2. **Some claims about the field come with a reason or mechanism; others are
   just asserted.**
   - With a reason: "System design and unit tests are more important now than
     ever. AI tries to find shortcuts and will often skip important things..."
   - Without: "AI is here to stay and it will only get better." / "CS is in a
     rough spot now. Almost like a Journalist in 2012ish"

3. **Some comments only ask for information and add none.**
   - "Did you hear back yet about offer/rejection?"
   - "Is it worth doing 2d dp? How long did you prep for"

4. **Some comments are pure feeling: support, venting, a joke.**
   - "Don't give up yet we got thissss 😔"
   - "RIGHT CUZ THE AI IS SO USELESS 😭 I didn't realize the ai can't generate
     tests..."

## Already looks hard

- Comment 1 vs 2: "I had mine Tuesday & still haven't heard back too" is
  first-hand but has almost no detail. Is that an experience report?
- Comment 2 vs 4: the "RIGHT CUZ THE AI IS SO USELESS" comment vents *and*
  reports a specific thing that happened (0 tests written on the first AI
  question).
